About the Project

A Public record, kept by the people who live here

Anyone in Ogoniland can report an oil spill, a gas flare, polluted water or dump waste. Every report is public and HYPREP follow each report until it is resolved and closed.

What each status means

New Received, buy not yet reviewed.
New This is currently being reviewed..
Resolved Reviewed and resolved. The Admin remark says how.
Not an incident Checked, and it was not an incident.
